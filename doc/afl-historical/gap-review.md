# Season-gap player review — outcomes

During the afltables historical backfill, same-name players were auto-linked
across a career gap on the assumption they were one person returning. A review
of every player with a **4+ year internal season gap** confirmed most were
genuine career interruptions, and identified nine same-name collisions that were
split into separate records.

The narratives below are the facts we established for each — kept here as a
record, and as candidate content should a player biography/notes column ever be
added to `player_season`.

## Split into separate players

| Player | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Aaron Black | **Three players.** North Melbourne (2011–15); Geelong (2017–18); West Coast (2022) — the latter a long-time West Perth star and 2014 Sandover medallist, signed as a COVID top-up and debuting at 29 for a single game. |
| Alwyn Davey | **Two players (father & son).** Alwyn Davey Sr, Essendon 2007–13; Alwyn Davey Jr, debuted for Essendon 2023. |
| Joel Smith | **Two players.** Hawthorn's Joel Smith (1999–2007) and Melbourne's Joel Smith (2017–23) are unrelated. |
| Josh Smith | **Two players.** Both played for Collingwood but are different people — a rookie defender (2008–10) and a mature-age VFL recruit (2016–19). |
| Matthew Kennedy | **Two players.** Matthew Kennedy (St Kilda, 1998–2001) and Matthew Kennedy (GWS/Carlton, 2015 draft onward, AFL 2016–present). |
| Ryan Davis | **Two players.** West Coast's Ryan Davis (2008–09) and Gold Coast's Ryan Davis (2016–17). |
| Sam Butler | **Two players.** Sam Butler (West Coast, 2004–17) and Sam Butler (Hawthorn, debuted 2022). |
| Tom Murphy | **Two players.** The Hawthorn/Gold Coast defender is one player; North Melbourne's Tom Murphy (2018, 2020) is a different player. |
| Will Hamill | **Two players.** Brisbane's Will Hamill (2007) is not Adelaide's Will Hamill (debut 2020). |
